# Dental assistant manual

You work chairside with the dentist: you record the findings the dentist calls out, prepare procedure records and
the materials used, and upload images. You cannot sign examinations, diagnoses, treatment plans or procedure
records, and you cannot create or change treatment plans. Read [getting-started.md](getting-started.md) first.
Your clinic may change what your role allows.

Your menu: **Dashboard**, **Patients**, **Waiting list**, **Appointments**, **Calendar**.

## Opening the examination

From the *Waiting list*, open the patient the dentist is seeing, then the dentist's examination. You can read every
tab. What you can change depends on the tab.

## Charting for the dentist

On **Chart**, while the examination is open (not signed):

1. Click the tooth the dentist names.
2. **Whole tooth** or **Surfaces**, then tap the surfaces (M, D, O or I, B, L).
3. Choose the finding (the code shown before each name is the symbol on the chart), add a note if the dentist
   asks, **Record finding**.

Read the finding back to the dentist from the tooth panel. A slip made in this examination can be removed; once
the examination is signed, only the dentist can mark a finding as entered in error.

On **Periodontal**, enter the six sites per tooth as the dentist calls them (probing depth / gingival margin in
whole millimetres, B for bleeding, P for plaque) and **Save**. Leave a site blank if it was not measured.

## Procedure records and materials

On **Procedures**, **Record a procedure** for the dentist (from the treatment plan, or not planned), choose who
performed it and who assisted, and fill in the notes the dentist dictates. Under **Materials used**, search the
stock list and add what was used (composite, anaesthetic cartridges, burs…), then **Save materials**. Instruments
are never consumed. The dentist checks the record and signs it; stock is taken only then.

## Images

On **Images**, **Upload** the radiograph or photograph with its kind, the teeth or region and the time it was
taken. If you upload the wrong image, tell the dentist or the imaging lead: they withdraw it with a reason.

## What you will not see

Signing buttons, the treatment plan editor and billing. If a button you expect is missing, ask the clinic
administrator. The system enforces the same limits on the server, so a missing button is never just hidden.
