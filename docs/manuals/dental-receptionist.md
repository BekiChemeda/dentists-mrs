# Dental receptionist manual

Everything in the [receptionist manual](receptionist.md) applies: registration, duplicate checks, the waiting
list, check-in, completing visits. This page covers what is different in a dental clinic. You see the patient's
details and appointments, never the dental chart, notes, diagnoses or images.

## Registering and checking in

- One record per patient, with one MRN, for everything. Never register a second record for dental work.
- **Add to waiting list** sends the patient to the dentists. Choose a dentist, or *First available dentist*.
  A dental clinic normally works without triage, so the patient goes straight to *Waiting for dentists*.

## Appointments

- Choose the **appointment type** (new patient examination, filling, extraction, root canal session, crown fitting,
  scaling, review…). It sets the usual length and the default chair; change either if needed.
- Choose the **dentist** and the **chair**. The system refuses a second booking for the same dentist or chair at the
  same time.
- A course of treatment is several appointments, one per visit. Book each visit separately; the dentist links them
  to the treatment plan.
- Rescheduling and cancelling need a reason; the history is kept.

## Recalls and follow-ups

*Recalls* lists reviews and recalls that are due, overdue or booked: post-operative reviews, root canal sessions,
crown fittings, periodontal reviews, preventive recalls.

- **Book** an appointment for the recall.
- Log each phone call under **Contact attempts** (reached, no answer, wrong number…).
- **Postpone** (with a new date), or mark **Declined**, or **Unreachable** once at least one attempt is logged.

Do not mention the reason for the recall in text messages or voicemail: say only that the clinic would like to
book a visit.

## Completing the visit

After the dentist signs and the cashier takes payment, complete the visit from *Waiting list › Completed visits*
as usual. The dialog lists anything still open: an unsigned dental examination or procedure record (ask the
dentist), or an unpaid balance.
