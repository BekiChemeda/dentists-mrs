# Nurse manual

You triage each patient: record the complaint, vital signs and allergies, set the priority, and send
the patient to the right department. Read [getting-started.md](getting-started.md) first.

Your menu: **Dashboard**, **Patients**, **Waiting list**, **Appointments**, **Calendar**, **Triage**.

## 1. Find the next patient

*Waiting list › **Waiting for triage*** (the **Triage** menu item opens the same list). Emergencies
and urgent patients are at the top; then the order of arrival. Click **Call next** to call the next
patient, or **Call** on a particular row.

## 2. Triage

Click **Triage** on the patient's row.

1. **Assessment**
   - **Main complaint** and **Duration** (for example "3 days"). Both are required.
   - **Relevant symptoms**.
   - **Current medications**: filled in from the patient's record; correct it if it has changed.
   - **General appearance**.
2. **Allergies**: ask the patient every time. The form shows what is already recorded; tick that you
   checked, or record **none known**. New allergies are added to the patient's record.
3. **Measurements**: **Systolic BP**, **Diastolic BP**, **Temperature**, **Heart rate**,
   **Respiratory rate**, **Oxygen saturation**, **Weight**, **Height**, **Pain score**,
   **Level of consciousness**, **Receiving oxygen**.
   - Readings outside the normal range for the patient's age are flagged as you type.
   - **Critical readings** are shown in red with the advice to set the priority to Emergency. When
     you save, the doctors are alerted.
   - Values that are impossible (for example a temperature of 60 °C) are refused; check and retype.
4. **Priority**: **Routine**, **Urgent** or **Emergency**.
5. **Triage notes** for the doctor, if needed.
6. **Triage to**: the department the patient goes to (for example Adult EOPD, Pediatric EOPD,
   OPD 1, OPD 2, Labour ward; the clinic administrator maintains this list).
7. Click **Save and send**. The patient moves to *Waiting for doctors* in that department.

## 3. Repeat measurements

To record vital signs again later the same day (for example after treatment), use **Record vital
signs** on the waiting list row. The earlier measurement is kept; the chart shows both with times.

## 4. While patients wait

Watch the waiting list. If a waiting patient gets worse, record new vital signs and change the
priority (**Set priority**). The waiting time column helps you spot people who have waited too long.

## 5. Looking at a patient's record

You can open any patient's record (**Patients**, or `/` search) to see past visits, vital signs,
allergies and verified laboratory results. You can correct demographic details with **Edit details**.
Clinical notes are written by doctors; you can read them but not change them.

## If the system is down

Use the paper triage form. Enter the measurements when the system is back, using **Record vital
signs**; the time saved will be the time of entry, so write the real time in the notes.
