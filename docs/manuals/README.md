# Clinic MRS manuals

One manual per job. Each one covers what that person does every day, step by step, and what to do
when something unusual happens. Staff only need their own manual; the menu on the left of the
screen shows only what their role may use.

| Manual | For |
| --- | --- |
| [Getting started](getting-started.md) | Everyone: signing in, passwords, two-step sign-in, finding a patient, light and dark mode |
| [Receptionist](receptionist.md) | Registration, appointments, the waiting list, closing visits |
| [Nurse](nurse.md) | Triage and vital signs |
| [Doctor](doctor.md) | Consultations, diagnoses, tests, prescriptions, referrals and certificates |
| [Laboratory](laboratory.md) | Specimens, results, verification, corrections and reports |
| [Pharmacy](pharmacy.md) | Dispensing, stock, the controlled-medicine register |
| [Cashier](cashier.md) | Invoices, payments, receipts, discounts, refunds, cash-up |
| [Accountant](accountant.md) | Financial review and reports (read only) |
| [Clinic administrator](clinic-administrator.md) | Staff, departments, prices, catalogues, approvals, reports, clinic settings and logo |
| [System administrator](system-administrator.md) | Installation, accounts and roles, security, backups, health, email, upgrades |
| [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md) | What to do when something does not work |
| [Maintenance and support](maintenance-and-support.md) | Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly checks; how support works |

The short [user guide](../user-guide.md) is a one-page summary of a normal visit.

## Conventions in these manuals

- **Bold** words are the names of buttons, menu items and fields exactly as they appear on screen.
- *Menu › Page › Tab* is the path to a screen, e.g. *Waiting list › Waiting for triage*.
- Every person signs in with their own account. Never share an account or use someone else's
  session: the audit log records who did what, and that record protects you.
- All examples use invented patients. Never copy real patient information into training material,
  screenshots or messages.
